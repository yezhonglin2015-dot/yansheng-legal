Our Heritage

A lineage written in silk and ink.

「 衣 冠 之 美 ， 谓 之 华 。 」

Yansheng is a contemporary maison rooted in the imperial wardrobes of the Tang, Song, and Ming.

Every piece begins on the loom in Suzhou, where seventh-generation weavers thread mulberry silk one warp at a time. We refuse machine embroidery, refuse synthetic dye, refuse haste. What we offer instead is a garment your grandchildren will wear.

From the cloud-shouldered hanfu of the Tang court to the architectural qipao of 1930s Shanghai, our atelier reinterprets these forms for a global wardrobe — without diluting a single stitch of their origin.

The House Code · 匠 心

Four disciplines, no shortcuts.

一

Mulberry Silk

Grade-6A silk from the Taihu basin, handloomed in widths unchanged since 1782.

二

Suzhou Embroidery

Su Xiu masters using needles thinner than a hair — every motif placed, never printed.

三

Plant Dyes

Indigo, gardenia, sappanwood. Colours that deepen with time, never fade against it.

四

Made-to-Measure

120 hours of hand-finishing per garment. Numbered, signed, registered to the wearer.
