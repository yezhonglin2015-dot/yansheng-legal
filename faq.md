# 常见问题 — 颜圣 YanSheng

---

## 关于评分标准

**颜圣用的是什么评分标准？**
颜圣基于东亚美学标准评分，而非西方标准。这意味着评分参考的是在东亚文化语境中被广泛认可的面部比例和五官和谐度基准——三庭五眼比例、面部宽长比、五官相互协调程度等。

**What beauty standard does YanSheng use?**
YanSheng scores faces against East Asian aesthetic benchmarks, not Western ones. The evaluation criteria are calibrated to the facial proportions, harmony ratios, and feature balance standards that are recognized as attractive within East Asian cultural contexts.

**8分是什么意思？7分呢？**
总分10分制，精确到小数点后一位。大致参考范围：9.0以上属于极具视觉冲击力的面孔；8.0–8.9属于显著高于平均水平；7.0–7.9属于高于平均；6.0–6.9属于平均水平；5.9以下说明存在较明显的比例或和谐度问题。大多数人的分数集中在6.5到8.0之间。

**What does a score of 8 mean? What about 7?**
Scores are out of 10, precise to one decimal. General reference: 9.0+ is a face with strong visual impact; 8.0–8.9 is significantly above average; 7.0–7.9 is above average; 6.0–6.9 is average; below 5.9 indicates notable proportion or harmony issues. Most people score between 6.5 and 8.0.

**AI能客观评价颜值吗？**
AI可以客观测量面部比例，并将其与预定义的美学标准进行对比。这比朋友的主观评价或社交媒体的点赞数更一致，但它仍然是一个标准下的评估，不是绝对真理。颜圣的价值在于给你一个结构化的参照系——知道自己在哪些维度上有优势、哪些有空间，比一个模糊的"你很好看"更有用。

**Can AI objectively evaluate attractiveness?**
AI can objectively measure facial proportions and compare them against predefined aesthetic standards. This is more consistent than subjective opinions from friends or social media metrics, but it is still an evaluation within a specific standard, not an absolute truth. YanSheng's value is giving you a structured reference frame — knowing which dimensions are your strengths and which have room for improvement is more useful than a vague "you're attractive."

## 关于分析内容

**颜圣分析哪些面部特征？**
颜圣测量23个面部维度，包括：面部宽长比、三庭比例、五眼比例、眼形分类、眼距、鼻宽与眼距之比、鼻长与面中部之比、唇部上下比例、唇宽与面宽之比、人中长度、颧骨位置与饱满度、下颌线清晰度、下颌角、下颏长度与投影、面部对称性、肤色色调分类等。

**What facial features does YanSheng analyze?**
YanSheng measures 23 facial dimensions including: face width-to-length ratio, three-zone vertical balance, five-section horizontal balance, eye shape classification, inter-eye spacing, nose width to inter-eye distance ratio, nose length to mid-face ratio, lip upper-to-lower volume ratio, lip width to face width ratio, philtrum length, cheekbone position and fullness, jawline definition, jaw angle, chin length and projection, facial symmetry, and skin undertone classification.

**男生和女生的分析有什么不同？**
男性分析更侧重骨相清晰度：下颌线定义分是男性的核心评分维度，颧骨突出度和整体骨相锐利感权重更高。女性分析更侧重五官和谐度和面部柔和度：苹果肌饱满度对女性是核心评分项，唇部比例和眼形权重更高。改善方向建议也完全不同——女性以妆容技巧、发型、色彩为主；男性以发型、护肤、面部训练、造型选择为主。

**How does the analysis differ for men and women?**
Male analysis weights structural sharpness more heavily: jawline definition is the primary male scoring dimension, with cheekbone prominence and overall bone sharpness weighted higher. Female analysis weights feature harmony and softness more heavily: cheek fullness is a core female scoring dimension, with lip ratio and eye shape weighted higher. Improvement directions are entirely different — women receive makeup technique, hairstyle, and color direction; men receive hairstyle, skincare, facial exercise, and styling direction.

**颜圣会分析皮肤吗？**
会。颜圣通过面部照片分析肤色色调（暖色、冷色或中性），并将皮肤清晰度作为整体评分的一个组成部分。皮肤分析用于生成色彩搭配建议——哪些服装色系适合你、哪些粉底色号方向最自然。颜圣不诊断皮肤疾病或医疗状况。

**Does YanSheng analyze skin?**
Yes. YanSheng analyzes skin undertone (warm, cool, or neutral) from the facial photo and includes skin clarity as a component of the overall score. The skin analysis is used to generate color recommendations — which clothing palettes suit you and which foundation shade direction looks most natural. YanSheng does not diagnose skin conditions or medical issues.

## 关于使用方式

**扫描需要多长时间？**
扫描过程约30–60秒。App会引导你完成5个面部姿势（正面、左侧偏转、右侧偏转、轻微仰头、微笑），确认检测到每个姿势后自动捕捉帧。AI分析通常在30秒内完成。

**How long does the scan take?**
The scan takes approximately 30–60 seconds. The app guides you through 5 facial poses (front, left yaw, right yaw, slight upward pitch, smile), automatically capturing frames as each pose is detected. AI analysis typically completes within 30 seconds.

**在什么光线下扫描效果最好？**
均匀的自然光或白色室内光效果最好。避免：强烈的顶光（会产生阴影遮盖面部特征）、逆光（背对窗户）、彩色光源（会影响肤色分析）、昏暗环境（降低图像清晰度影响测量精度）。正面朝向光源，距离约40–60厘米。

**What lighting works best for scanning?**
Even natural light or white indoor lighting works best. Avoid: strong overhead light (creates shadows that obscure features), backlight (facing away from a window), colored light sources (skews skin tone analysis), and dim environments (reduces image clarity and measurement precision). Face toward the light source directly, at approximately 40–60 cm distance.

**需要卸妆才能扫描吗？**
不需要。颜圣分析你当前的面部状态，包括化妆状态。如果你想了解自己素颜的基础评分，可以在素颜状态下扫描；如果你想测试某个妆容是否适合你的脸型，可以在化妆后扫描。两种方式都有参考价值。

**Do I need to remove makeup before scanning?**
No. YanSheng analyzes your face in its current state, including with makeup. If you want to understand your baseline score without makeup, scan bare-faced. If you want to test whether a particular makeup look suits your face, scan with it on. Both are useful.

## 关于改善方向

**颜圣会推荐整容或医美吗？**
不会。颜圣的所有改善方向建议都是非医疗性的：发型、妆容技巧、护肤、色彩搭配、造型选择、面部训练。App不推荐任何手术或注射类医美项目。

**Does YanSheng recommend cosmetic surgery or medical procedures?**
No. All improvement directions from YanSheng are non-medical: hairstyle, makeup technique, skincare, color choices, styling, and facial exercises. The app does not recommend any surgical or injectable procedures.

**改善方向是通用建议还是针对我个人的？**
完全针对你个人。颜圣的AI分析你的具体测量数据，识别你最大的提升空间，并据此给出单一的最佳改善方向——不是通用的"你应该做这个"，而是"基于你的具体面部比例，这个方向对你的提升效益最大"。

**Are improvement directions generic or personalized?**
Fully personalized. YanSheng's AI analyzes your specific measurements, identifies your single largest improvement opportunity, and generates direction based on that — not generic "you should do this" advice, but "based on your specific facial proportions, this direction has the highest improvement leverage for you."

**面部训练真的有效吗？**
面部肌肉和身体其他部位的肌肉一样，可以通过训练增强和塑形。有研究表明，定期面部运动可以改善面部肌肉张力，减缓某些与年龄相关的松弛，并改善面部轮廓的清晰度。颜圣的12个练习有动画示范，坚持6–8周通常可以看到可见的变化，尤其是在下颌线和面颊区域。

**Do facial exercises actually work?**
Facial muscles, like muscles elsewhere in the body, can be strengthened and reshaped through training. Research suggests that regular facial exercise can improve facial muscle tone, slow certain age-related sagging, and improve the clarity of facial contours. YanSheng's 12 exercises are animated, and most people notice visible changes after 6–8 weeks of consistent practice, particularly in the jawline and cheek areas.

## 关于隐私和数据

**我的面部数据会被存储吗？**
会，但仅在你使用App期间，且不与你的任何身份信息绑定。你的扫描结果存储在Firebase（Google Cloud）中，只与一个随机生成的匿名会话ID关联，没有你的姓名、邮箱或Apple ID。你可以随时在App内删除你的所有扫描数据。

**Is my face data stored?**
Yes, but only for as long as you use the app, and never linked to your identity. Your scan is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud) linked only to an anonymous random session ID — no name, email, or Apple ID. You can delete all your scan data at any time from within the app.

**颜圣会把我的脸用于AI训练吗？**
不会。通过API提交给Anthropic的图像，根据Anthropic的API数据政策，不会在未经单独授权的情况下被用于模型训练。颜圣不会将你的面部图像用于任何训练目的。

**Will YanSheng use my face to train AI?**
No. Images submitted to Anthropic via API are not used for model training without separate consent, per Anthropic's API data policy. YanSheng does not use your facial images for any training purpose.

**App会识别我是谁吗？**
不会。颜圣不进行身份验证、人脸识别或任何形式的用户身份关联。你的面部数据永远不会与你的姓名、账户或联系方式绑定。

**Does the app identify who I am?**
No. YanSheng does not perform identity verification, facial recognition, or any form of user identity association. Your face is never linked to your name, account, or contact details.

## 关于评分可靠性

**每次扫描结果一样吗？**
在相同光线和姿势条件下，同一张脸的评分会非常接近（通常在0.2分以内）。影响分数的因素包括：光线变化（阴影位置影响五官测量精度）、扫描时的面部表情（建议保持放松中性表情）、摄像头角度（正对摄像头效果最准确）。

**Are scores consistent across multiple scans?**
Under the same lighting and pose conditions, scores for the same face are very close — typically within 0.2 points. Factors that affect scores include: lighting variation (shadow position affects measurement precision), facial expression during scan (relaxed neutral expression is recommended), and camera angle (directly facing the camera produces the most accurate results).

**颜圣的评分和"真实颜值"一致吗？**
颜圣测量的是面部比例和特征与东亚美学标准的符合程度。这与"真实颜值"高度相关，因为面部比例是人类感知吸引力的最强预测因素之一。但吸引力也受其他因素影响：气质、表情管理、个人风格、身材比例、声音、社交能力等。颜圣专注于面部，给你这一个维度的精准数据。

**Does YanSheng's score align with "real-world" attractiveness?**
YanSheng measures how closely facial proportions and features match East Asian aesthetic standards. This correlates strongly with real-world attractiveness perception, because facial proportion is one of the strongest predictors of perceived attractiveness in human research. But attractiveness is also influenced by other factors: presence, expression management, personal style, body proportion, voice, social skills, and more. YanSheng focuses on the face and gives you precise data on that one dimension.
