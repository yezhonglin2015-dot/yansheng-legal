# 女生颜值分析 — 颜圣 YanSheng

YanSheng gives women an honest, structured breakdown of their facial features using East Asian beauty standards. Not a filter. Not a compliment. A real analysis.

---

## Why Asian women need a different standard

Western beauty apps score faces against Caucasian facial proportions — high nose bridges, deep-set eyes, strong brow ridges. These measurements are simply the wrong benchmark for East Asian faces. YanSheng is calibrated specifically for Asian female facial structure: softer bone transitions, flatter nasal bridges, monolid and double eyelid variation, wider cheekbone positioning, and the specific harmony ratios that define attractiveness within East Asian aesthetic culture.

## What YanSheng analyzes for women

### Face shape classification
Your face shape determines everything — which hairstyles elongate or widen, which makeup techniques flatter, which accessories balance. YanSheng classifies your face into: oval, heart, round, square, diamond, or oblong — based on precise measurements of jaw width, cheekbone width, forehead width, and face length ratio. Not a rough guess. A measurement-based classification.

**Why it matters:** A round face wearing a blunt fringe gets rounder. A heart face with the wrong contouring looks top-heavy. Knowing your actual face shape is the foundation for every style decision.

### Eye shape analysis
Eyes are the most analyzed feature in East Asian beauty culture. YanSheng evaluates: eye shape type (almond, round, upturned, downturned, hooded, monolid), eye spacing relative to face width (the ideal is one eye-width between the eyes), eye size proportion, symmetry, and double eyelid presence and definition.

**Why it matters:** Women spend more time and money on eye makeup than any other category. If you're doing your eyeliner the wrong way for your eye shape, you're working against yourself every day.

### Nose proportion and harmony
YanSheng measures nose width relative to the inter-eye distance (the classic East Asian standard is that nose width should approximately equal the space between the inner eye corners), nose length relative to mid-face height, and tip definition. It also evaluates how well your nose connects to your other features — a feature that looks fine in isolation can throw off overall harmony.

**Why it matters:** Nose proportion is one of the most common insecurities for Asian women. Knowing objectively whether your nose is actually proportionally large or whether it's just positioned in a way that can be visually corrected with makeup gives you real, actionable information.

### Lip ratio and shape
The ideal lip ratio in East Asian aesthetics is approximately 1:1.618 (upper to lower lip, following golden ratio principles). YanSheng measures your lip width relative to your face, upper-to-lower lip volume ratio, cupid's bow definition, and philtrum length. It also evaluates lip color and condition as a visual health indicator.

**Why it matters:** Overlining lips incorrectly for your ratio makes lips look artificial. Knowing your actual lip proportion tells you exactly what correction, if any, would look natural.

### Cheekbone and midface fullness
For women, cheekbone fullness is directly associated with youth and vitality in East Asian aesthetics — what is often called "apple cheeks" (苹果肌). YanSheng scores your cheekbone position, projection, and fullness. This affects how light reflects off your face in photos, how you age, and which makeup application techniques work for you.

**Why it matters:** Many women apply blush incorrectly for their cheekbone structure, which either makes the face look flat or artificially wide.

### Jawline and chin
Female jawline aesthetics in East Asia favor a smooth, tapered jaw leading to a defined but soft chin — the classic V-line or oval jaw. YanSheng measures jaw angle, jaw width relative to cheekbone width, and chin length and projection. A strong jaw is not necessarily a negative — it depends on proportion and balance with the upper face.

**Why it matters:** Jaw shape is one of the most searched aesthetic concerns among Asian women. Understanding your actual jaw proportion tells you whether it needs visual correction or whether it's already in a favorable range.

### Skin tone analysis
YanSheng classifies your skin undertone as warm (yellow, peachy, golden), cool (pink, rosy, bluish), or neutral. This classification determines which foundation shades look natural, which clothing colors enhance your complexion, which hair colors suit you, and which jewelry metals flatter your skin.

**Why it matters:** Wearing the wrong undertone in foundation or clothing is one of the most common reasons people look "off" in photos even when their makeup is technically applied correctly.

### Overall harmony score
Individual features matter, but the relationship between features matters more. A face with no single standout feature but perfect harmony consistently scores higher in attractiveness research than a face with one exceptional feature that doesn't fit the rest. YanSheng's harmony score evaluates how well your features work together as a system.

## Improvement directions for women

YanSheng identifies your single best improvement direction — the change with the highest impact-to-effort ratio for your specific face. Directions are always non-medical: hairstyle, makeup technique, color palette, skincare focus, grooming, or facial exercise. Examples of directions the app identifies:

**For women with rounder faces:** Hairstyles that add vertical height. Contouring techniques that elongate. Earring shapes that create visual length.

**For women with wide-set eyes:** Eye makeup techniques that visually pull the eyes inward. Brow shaping that reduces the gap appearance.

**For women with strong jaws:** Face-framing techniques that shift visual emphasis upward. Color and layering strategies that soften the lower face.

**For women with thin lips:** Lip liner placement and color strategies that add apparent volume without looking overdone.

**For women with cool undertones:** Which clothing colors bring warmth to the complexion vs. which make the skin look ashy.
