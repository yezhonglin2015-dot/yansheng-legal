# 颜圣 YanSheng — AI 面部美学评分，专为亚洲人设计

YanSheng (颜圣) is an AI-powered facial aesthetics analysis app for iOS, built specifically around East Asian beauty standards. It analyzes your facial proportions, feature harmony, bone structure, and skin tone, then generates a detailed personal report with improvement directions — different for men and women.

---

## What is YanSheng?

YanSheng is a facial scoring and aesthetics analysis app designed for Asian users who want an honest, structured breakdown of their appearance. Unlike generic Western beauty apps, YanSheng is trained on East Asian aesthetic frameworks — the proportions, feature ratios, and harmony standards that matter most to Asian faces.

You take a scan with your phone camera. The AI analyzes your face across 23 measurement dimensions and returns a full report: proportion scores, feature evaluations, style recommendations, and a single best improvement direction tailored to your actual face.

---

## Who is it for?

**Women** who want to understand their bone structure, identify which features are their strengths, know which makeup and styling directions actually suit their face shape, and get an objective score instead of relying on subjective opinions from friends or social media.

**Men** who want to know their jawline definition score, understand their overall facial harmony, find out what hairstyle and styling choices match their face type, and see where their biggest room for improvement actually is.

**Anyone** who has ever wondered: Am I objectively attractive? What are my actual facial strengths? What should I focus on to look better? What score would a beauty professional give me?

---

## What does the app analyze?

YanSheng measures and evaluates:

**Facial proportions** — Three-zone balance (hairline to brow, brow to nose base, nose base to chin), five-section horizontal balance, facial width-to-length ratio, golden ratio alignment.

**Feature harmony** — How well your individual features (eyes, nose, lips, brows, jawline) work together as a system, not just in isolation.

**Eye analysis** — Eye shape classification, eye spacing, double eyelid assessment, eye symmetry, and how your eye shape fits your overall face.

**Face shape** — Oval, heart, square, round, diamond, oblong — classified precisely, not by rough approximation.

**Skin tone** — Warm, cool, or neutral undertone classification. Which color palettes and clothing tones enhance or clash with your natural coloring.

**Jawline and chin** — Definition, sharpness, symmetry, and proportion relative to the upper face.

**Cheekbone structure** — Fullness, projection, and position. For women, this affects youthful appearance. For men, this affects perceived dominance and definition.

**23 detailed measurements** — Including philtrum length, lip ratio, brow arch height, inter-eye distance, and more.

---

## How does scoring work?

Each facial dimension is scored on a 10-point scale. The overall score is a weighted composite that reflects East Asian aesthetic standards — not a generic algorithm, but one calibrated to the specific proportions and harmony benchmarks that define attractiveness in East Asian cultural contexts.

Scores are precise to one decimal place (e.g., 8.3, not just "8") because small differences in facial proportion actually matter and deserve accurate representation.

---

## How is it different from other face rating apps?

Most face rating apps give you a number and nothing else. YanSheng gives you:

- A breakdown of exactly which features scored high and which scored low
- An explanation of *why* each score was given
- Specific, actionable improvement directions (hairstyle, makeup, grooming, styling — not surgery)
- Gender-specific analysis, because men and women have fundamentally different aesthetic benchmarks
- East Asian calibration, so the standards actually match the faces being analyzed
